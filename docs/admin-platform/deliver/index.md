# Deliver

The **Deliver** section contains the tools you use once your experience is live and learners are actively participating. While **Setup** is about designing the program, Deliver is about running it — managing feedback, communicating with participants, tracking progress, and publishing reports.

All Deliver tools apply to the **currently selected experience**. Check the top bar to confirm you are in the right experience context before making changes.

---

## What Deliver covers

| Section | Route | Purpose |
|---------|-------|---------|
| **Feedback** | `/feedback` | Manage the feedback pipeline — submissions, reviewer assignment, status, Team 360 |
| **Reports** | `/reports` | Build and publish customised progress reports |
| **Comms / Chat** | `/comms` | Send messages, manage channels, schedule communications |
| **Progress** | `/progress` | Skills growth metrics and configurable KPI tiles |

!!! note "Users & Teams are under Setup"
    Enrolments and team management are accessed from the **Users** link in the sidebar, not directly under Deliver.

---

## Sub-section guides

### [Feedback](feedback.md)

The Feedback hub gives you a unified view of the assessment submission pipeline. Assign and reassign reviewers, track the completion matrix, and publish Team 360 peer review reports — all from one screen.

### [Reports](reports.md)

Use the drag-and-drop report builder to compose rich, customised reports for individual learners. Choose from 6 section types and 5 chart types. Preview the report before publishing it via each learner's report card.

### [Comms / Chat](chat.md)

Communicate with all participants or subgroups via the Chat tab. Send broadcast announcements, run direct messages, or schedule messages for future delivery. The Email and Notifications tabs provide additional communication channels.

### [Progress](progress.md)

Track cohort-level skills development with the Skills Growth chart, and build configurable KPI metric tiles to monitor what matters most for your experience.

## Typical delivery workflow

Here is how coordinators typically use the Deliver section during an active experience:

1. **Daily**: check the **Feedback → Pipeline** for new submissions and overdue reviews.
2. **Weekly**: review the **Progress** screen to spot learners falling behind.
3. **Mid-experience**: use **Comms → Chat** to send check-in messages to teams.
4. **End of milestone**: publish **Team 360** reports from the Feedback → Team 360 tab.
5. **End of experience**: build and share the final **Report** via Reports → Editor.

!!! tip
    Bookmark the Feedback screen — it is the highest-traffic screen for most coordinators during an active delivery.
