# Getting Started

Welcome to the Practera Admin Platform. This section walks you through the essentials: signing in, understanding the layout, and switching between the experiences you manage.

You do not need technical knowledge to use the Admin Platform. If you have been a Practera coordinator or administrator before, most tasks work the same way — v2.6 mainly improves how you navigate between them.

## The basics in three steps

### 1. Log in

Access the Admin Platform through the **Practera Login App** — either at [login.practera.com](https://login.practera.com) or your institution's custom login URL. Enter your email, click the magic link sent to your inbox, and you will arrive at the Admin Platform.

See [Logging in](logging-in.md) for full details, including passkey setup and troubleshooting.

### 2. Navigate

Once logged in, you will see:

- A **top bar** showing your institution, the current experience (if selected), and your user menu
- A **left sidebar** with Deliver, Setup, and Expert sections
- The **main content area** for whichever page you have open

The sidebar can be collapsed to give you more room. Click the institution badge in the top bar to access institution-wide pages like Experiences and Settings.

See [Navigation](navigation.md) for a complete tour of every menu and shortcut.

### 3. Switch experiences

Most admin tasks happen in the context of a **single experience** — one program or cohort. Before reviewing feedback or editing the designer, make sure the correct experience is selected in the top bar.

Open **Experiences** from the institution menu (or go to `/experiences`), then click the card for the program you want to manage. The top bar updates to show that experience's name and status.

See [Switching experiences](switching-experiences.md) for details on status badges, going live, and returning to institution view.

## Before you dive in

!!! note "Your role determines what you see"
    Coordinators see experience-level tools in Deliver and Setup. Institution administrators also see institution settings, audit logs, and user management. Practera CS managers have additional options such as CS Manager, Super Badges, and Billing. If a menu item is missing, your administrator may need to adjust your role.

!!! tip "Start from the Dashboard"
    After switching into an experience, open **Dashboard** in the Deliver section for a snapshot of enrolment, submissions, and activity health. It is the best place to orient yourself before taking action.
