# Users & Enrolments

Managing people is one of the most important parts of running an experiential learning program. The Practera Admin Platform gives you two complementary ways to work with users: **per-experience enrolments** for day-to-day program delivery, and **institution-wide user management** for administrators who need a view across all programs.

This section explains how roles work, where to find user tools, and how to add participants to your experience.

---

## Where to manage users

You can manage users in two places, depending on what you need to do:

| Location | Best for |
|----------|----------|
| **Enrolments page** (Setup → Users, or `/enrolments`) | Adding learners, reviewers, and coordinators to the **current experience**; changing roles; bulk invites; CSV import |
| **Institution → Users tab** | Institution administrators managing users **across the whole institution** — viewing all accounts, assigning institution-level roles, and auditing access |

!!! note "Experience context matters"
    Most coordinators work from the **Enrolments** page while an experience is selected in the top bar. Institution-wide user management is available from the institution menu (click the institution badge in the top bar) and is typically restricted to **Admin** and **CS Admin** roles.

For step-by-step guides on enrolling participants and forming teams, see:

- [Enrolments](enrolments.md) — add, invite, import, and remove users in an experience
- [Teams](teams.md) — create teams and assign learners for peer review and collaboration

## Understanding roles

Every user in Practera has a **role** that determines what they can see and do. Roles apply at the experience level unless noted otherwise.

| Role | Who they are | What they do |
|------|--------------|--------------|
| **Learner** | Students, interns, participants | Complete activities, submit assessments, receive feedback, participate in team tasks |
| **Reviewer** | Mentors, industry experts, tutors | Review learner submissions, provide written feedback, and acknowledge completed reviews |
| **Coordinator** | Program managers, teaching staff | Manage the experience day to day — enrolments, teams, feedback pipeline, comms, and reports |
| **Admin** | Institution administrator | Manage institution settings, branding, audit logs, and users across all experiences at the institution |
| **CS Admin** | Practera customer success staff | Platform-wide access for support, billing, and cross-institution administration |

!!! tip "One person, multiple roles"
    A user can hold different roles in different experiences. For example, someone might be a **Learner** in one internship program and a **Reviewer** in another. When you enrol someone, you assign their role **for that experience only**.

!!! warning "Admin vs Coordinator"
    **Coordinators** manage a single experience — they cannot change institution settings or access other programs unless they are enrolled there too. **Admins** operate at the institution level and can see all experiences and institution-wide user lists. If someone needs to run programs but not manage the institution, assign **Coordinator**, not Admin.

## How to add users

The fastest way to get participants into your program is through the **Enrolments** page. Make sure the correct experience is selected in the top bar, then open **Users** in the Setup section of the sidebar (or go directly to `/enrolments`).

You have three options:

### Add User (single enrolment)

Click **Add User**, enter the person's email address, select their role (Learner, Reviewer, or Coordinator), and click **Add**. If the person already has a Practera account, they are enrolled immediately. If not, they receive an **invitation email** with instructions to sign in.

### Bulk Invite

Click **Invite**, paste multiple email addresses (one per line or separated by commas), assign a role for the group, and click **Send**. Each invited user receives a **magic link** via email that lets them sign in and join the experience without needing a password.

### CSV Import

Click **Import**, download the provided template, fill in the **name**, **email**, and **role** columns for each participant, upload the file, review the preview, and confirm. CSV import is ideal when you already have a class list or roster from your student information system.

!!! tip "Start early"
    Add coordinators and reviewers before learners go live. That way your feedback pipeline and team assignments are ready when the first submissions arrive.
