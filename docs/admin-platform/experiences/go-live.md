# Going Live

**Going live** publishes your experience so enrolled learners can access it in the Practera App. Until an experience is Live, it remains in **Draft** — visible to coordinators and authors in the Admin Platform, but hidden from participants.

![Screenshot](../../assets/placeholder.png)

---

## Draft → Live workflow

When your experience is designed, tested, and ready for participants:

1. Confirm the correct experience is selected in the top bar
2. Click the amber **Go Live** button in the top bar, **or**
3. Open **Setup → Settings → General** and change **Status** to **Live**

Both paths perform the same action. The top bar button is the fastest option during final pre-launch checks.

!!! note "You need coordinator or administrator access"
    Only users with permission to manage experience settings can go live. If you do not see the Go Live button, ask your institution administrator to adjust your role.

---

## What "going live" means

When an experience goes live:

- **Enrolled learners** can open the program in the Practera App and begin activities
- **Scheduled notifications** (reminders, feedback alerts) start sending according to your notification settings
- **Due dates** on the experience calendar become active for on-track tracking on the Dashboard
- **Reviewers and mentors** assigned to the experience can receive submissions

Going live does **not** automatically enrol learners — you must still add participants via **Setup → Users**, CSV upload, or LTI roster sync before they can log in.

---

## Pre-launch checklist

Use this checklist before clicking **Go Live**:

| Step | Where to check |
|------|----------------|
| **Activities designed** | **Setup → Designer** — milestones, activities, and assessments are complete and published |
| **Enrolments set up** | **Setup → Users** or **Deliver → Enrolments** — learners, mentors, and reviewers are enrolled |
| **Notifications configured** | **Setup → Settings → Notifications** — reminder and feedback categories are enabled as needed |
| **Schedule configured** | **Setup → Schedule** — due dates align with your program calendar |
| **Test run with preview user** | Use the **Student Emulator** or a test account to walk through key activities as a learner |

!!! tip "Run a full learner journey"
    Log in as a preview or test user and complete at least one submission and one review cycle before go-live. This catches broken links, missing instructions, and misconfigured assessments early.

## After going live

Once published, the experience status updates across the Admin Platform:

| Before | After |
|--------|-------|
| Amber **Go Live** button | Green **Live** indicator (no longer clickable as an action) |
| **Draft** status badge (grey/amber) | **Live** status badge (green) on the Experiences list and top bar |

You can continue editing content while live — for example, fixing typos or adjusting due dates — but be mindful that learners may see changes immediately. For major structural changes, consider duplicating the experience for the next cohort instead.

Monitor the **Dashboard** in the first days after launch for enrolment counts, on-track metrics, and submission activity.

---

## Changing status later

| Status | When to use |
|--------|-------------|
| **Draft** | Pull an experience offline temporarily during major redesign (learners lose access) |
| **Inactive** | Program has ended; retain data but stop active delivery |
| **Archive** | Long-term storage; removes from the active Experiences list (see below) |

!!! warning "Taking a Live experience back to Draft removes learner access"
    Learners cannot open a Draft experience. Use this only when you intentionally need to pause the program. Communicate with participants before changing status.

---

## Archiving an experience

When a program has run its course and you no longer need it in day-to-day workflows:

1. Open the **Experiences** list from the institution menu
2. Open the card menu for the experience
3. Select **Archive**

Archiving **removes the experience from the active list** but **keeps all data** — submissions, reviews, enrolments, and reports remain available for export and audit.

!!! warning "Archived experiences cannot be re-activated without contacting support"
    Once archived, an experience cannot be returned to Live status through the Admin Platform. Contact [support@practera.com](mailto:support@practera.com) if you need to restore an archived program.

Prefer **Archive** over **Delete** when you may need historical data for accreditation, reporting, or GDPR audit trails.
