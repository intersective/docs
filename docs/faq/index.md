## 🚀 Getting Started

### How do I log in to Practera?
**Solution:** Clear your browser cache, try incognito mode, and verify your email address is correct. If login still fails, use the password reset feature or contact support for assistance. [Complete Login Guide →](../help-articles/onboarding/logging-in.md)

### I can't access my account
**Solution:** Check your email for registration confirmation, verify you're using the correct login URL, and ensure your account is active. For immediate assistance, contact customersuccess@practera.com.

### How do I set up my first experience?
**Solution:** Begin with experience selection, configure basic settings, add branding elements, then enroll participants. [Getting Started Guide →](../help-articles/onboarding/index.md) | [Branding Guide →](../help-articles/onboarding/branding-your-experience.md)

### Where can I preview my experience?
**Solution:** Access the preview mode in Experience Design to test all features before going live. [Preview Guide →](../help-articles/onboarding/previewing-your-experience.md)

### What do lock icons, red dots, and bookmarks mean?
**Solution:** Lock icons indicate restricted content that requires prerequisites to unlock. Red dots show unfinished activities that need completion. Bookmarks mark where learners last stopped. [Visual Cues Guide →](../help-articles/becoming-a-practera-power-user/visual-cues.md)

## 👥 Experience Management

### How do I enroll learners and experts?
**Solution:** Upload participant data via CSV for bulk enrollment, or add individual users through the management interface. [Bulk Enrollment →](../help-articles/essentials/bulk-enrolment-via-csv-upload.md) | [Individual Enrollment →](../help-articles/essentials/enrolling-learners-experts.md)

### How do I create and manage teams?
**Solution:** Configure team settings, establish team sizes, assign members, and manage team dynamics through the Teams interface. [Team Creation Guide →](../help-articles/essentials/creating-teams.md)

### How do I communicate with participants?
**Solution:** Send targeted announcements, schedule automated notifications, and utilize built-in messaging features for participant engagement. [Communication Guide →](../help-articles/essentials/communicating-with-experience-participants.md)

### How do I schedule due dates and milestones?
**Solution:** Establish timeline milestones, configure due dates, and manage scheduling across multiple cohorts using the scheduling tools. [Scheduling Guide →](../help-articles/essentials/scheduling-due-dates.md)

### How long should data be kept?
**Solution:** Data should be deleted after 2 years unless it is still being used for the purpose for which it was collected. The retention period starts from the experience start date set when going live. [Data Deletion Guide →](../help-articles/essentials/deleting-user-data.md)

### Who can delete user data?
**Solution:** All Institution Admins can delete data. This ensures that data deletion is controlled and only performed by authorized personnel. [Data Deletion Guide →](../help-articles/essentials/deleting-user-data.md)

### How do I delete an entire experience?
**Solution:** Navigate to Overview, select the experience, choose "more" then "Delete user data", review the data summary, confirm with the toggle, and begin the deletion process. A certificate will be available upon completion. [Complete Guide →](../help-articles/essentials/deleting-user-data.md)

### How do I delete an individual user's data?
**Solution:** Go to Overview > Users tab, select the user, click the trash bin icon, review the deletion details, and confirm deletion. A certificate will be generated for your records. [Complete Guide →](../help-articles/essentials/deleting-user-data.md)

### How do I verify data deletion?
**Solution:** Navigate to Overview > Deletion Records tab, enter the user's email address, and click "Verify Deletion" to view or regenerate the deletion certificate. [Complete Guide →](../help-articles/essentials/deleting-user-data.md)

## 🔧 Troubleshooting

### Certificates and badges not downloading
**Issue:** Certificate download links expire after a set period for security purposes.  
**Solution:** Request new download links from your instructor or contact support for immediate certificate re-issuance.

### Interface not responding or buttons not working
**Issue:** Browser compatibility or caching problems affecting functionality.  
**Solution:** Clear browser cache, disable browser extensions, try incognito mode, or switch browsers. Report persistent issues to technical support.

### Team members not participating
**Issue:** Team collaboration or engagement challenges.  
**Solution:** Contact your instructor to manage team dynamics, reassign team members, or process participant withdrawals as needed.

### Content not displaying correctly
**Issue:** Loading or rendering problems affecting content visibility.  
**Solution:** Ensure stable internet connection, refresh the page, or contact support if content consistently fails to load properly.

### Integration issues with LMS
**Issue:** Connectivity or synchronization problems with learning management systems.  
**Solution:** Verify LTI configuration settings, check user permissions, and ensure proper course mapping. Contact technical support for integration troubleshooting.
