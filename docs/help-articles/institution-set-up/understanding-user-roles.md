# Understanding User Roles


Learn about the different user types on Practera.
A Practera user is anyone with an administrator, author, coordinator, expert or learner license. These user roles can be divided into two groups: those who design and deliver experiences (administrators, authors, and coordinators) and those who participate in the experiences (learners and experts). Let’s take a closer look at each of these user types.
### Administrators

Administrators on Practera manage all aspects of an institutional portal, including branding and the ability to invite other administrators. This is the highest level of access on Practera and it has visibility over billing, account management, and all experiences inside an institution. Administrators access the [Author & Coordinator Interface](../onboarding/logging-in/) on Practera.
Administrators are also able to perform the same actions as authors, described below.
### Authors

Authors on Practera create, prepare, and edit experiences for learners and experts. They have access to one or a set of particular experiences and can also add, build, and create their own experiences on Practera (either by using the [Experience Library](../onboarding/exploring-the-experience-library/) or from scratch). Authors share experience access with other authors or coordinators and have the ability to invite learners and experts to any experience they are part of. Authors access the [Author & Coordinator Interface](../onboarding/logging-in/) on Practera.
When delivering their experience, authors can monitor the experience dashboard, launch interventions, facilitate feedback loops, assign other users to provide feedback, and support experts and learners through chat.
### Coordinators

Coordinators on Practera are able to support the delivery of experiences to learners and experts. However, coordinators are unable to edit or create experiences, which is their main difference from authors. Coordinators share experience access with authors or other coordinators and have the ability to invite learners and experts to any experience they are part of. Coordinators access the [Author & Coordinator Interface](../onboarding/logging-in/) on Practera.
When delivering an experience, coordinators can monitor the experience dashboard, launch interventions, facilitate feedback loops, assign other users to provide feedback, and support experts and learners through chat.
### Experts

Experts on Practera provide feedback to learners on their submissions or assessments. They are an essential part of the [experiential learning cycle](../onboarding/what-is-experiential-learning/#1-toc-title)! Experts can have their own instructions or learning content if it has been created by authors. They can access chat if they are placed in a team.
Experts access the [Learner & Expert Interface](../onboarding/logging-in/) on Practera.
### Learners

Learners on Practera are the main participants of the experiences created by authors! Learners have their own instructions or learning content provided by authors and navigate their experiential learning journey by completing activities and making assessment submissions along the way. They can access chat if they are placed in a team.
Learners access the [Learner & Expert Interface](../onboarding/logging-in/) on Practera.
